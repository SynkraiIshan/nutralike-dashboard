# Nutralike Admin Dashboard

## Executive Summary

The Nutralike Admin Dashboard is an enterprise-grade web application designed to streamline internal operations, manage raw nutraceutical ingredient catalogs, automate B2B quotation workflows, and provide actionable business intelligence. Built with modern, high-performance web standards, the platform offers a secure, intuitive, and highly responsive environment tailored for administrators, procurement managers, and sales executives.

---

## Core System Architecture & User Experience

The application has been engineered to deliver a premium user experience across all screen sizes, from desktop monitors to mobile devices.

*   **Responsive Navigation:** A dynamic sidebar system that expands for full desktop viewing and collapses into a clean, icon-only mode to maximize workspace on smaller screens. On mobile devices, a secure slide-out drawer provides instant access to all core modules.
*   **Brand Uniformity:** The interface incorporates official Nutralike branding, curated color palettes, and polished visual cues such as subtle glassmorphism and smooth micro-animations.
*   **Intuitive Feedback:** Every action, from logging in to creating complex quotations, is accompanied by instant visual feedback, success banners, and clear error handling to ensure seamless daily operations.

## Key Features & How They Work

### 1. Secure Authentication & Administrator Portal
Security is paramount in managing enterprise data. The portal begins with a highly secure login interface.

*   **Operational Workflow:** Authorized administrators access the system via their designated credentials. The system supports session retention ("Remember me") for up to seven days, eliminating the need for repeated daily logins on trusted devices.
*   **Access Protection:** Built-in security layers actively monitor active sessions. Unverified visitors attempting to access internal dashboard links are automatically redirected back to the secure login screen.

### 2. Real-Time Executive Dashboard
The primary landing screen provides leadership with an immediate snapshot of organizational health.

*   **Key Performance Indicators:** Instant visibility into total active ingredients, monthly quotation volume, client acquisition rates, and pending approval tasks.
*   **Live Activity Feed:** A dynamic audit log tracking recent actions across the organization, such as newly generated price quotes, updated batch prices, and recently imported files.
*   **Quick Summary Tables:** Immediate access to high-priority quotations requiring administrative review or urgent client follow-ups.

### 3. Intelligent Ingredient Management
A centralized repository for tracking raw materials, formulations, and active stock levels.

*   **Catalog Browsing:** Ingredients are organized with clear categorizations, SKU identifiers, unit pricing, and stock status indicators.
*   **Search and Filtering:** Administrators can instantly search through hundreds of items by name, category, or stock availability without waiting for page reloads.
*   **Stock Alerts:** Visual badges highlight ingredients that are running low in inventory, allowing procurement managers to reorder materials before shortages occur.

### 4. Smart Batch Uploads & Data Import
Manual data entry is prone to human error and time delays. The Uploads module automates the intake of supplier data and ingredient lists.

*   **Document Parsing:** Users can upload batch inventory files (such as Excel sheets or standardized CSV documents) directly into the secure upload zone.
*   **Real-Time Validation:** The system scans the document upon upload, verifies ingredient specifications, checks for duplicate entries, and confirms pricing structures before committing data to the active catalog.
*   **Audit History:** Every upload session is permanently logged with timestamps, file names, record counts, and status indicators for complete traceability.

### 5. Advanced B2B Quotation Wizard
Generating accurate, customized pricing quotes for business clients is the financial core of the platform.

*   **Step-by-Step Creation:** A guided workflow allows sales representatives to select target clients, compile necessary raw ingredients, define required formulation quantities, and apply custom packaging parameters.
*   **Dynamic Margin Calculation:** The system automatically calculates baseline raw material costs, applies recommended profit margins, and factors in packaging fees to compute final B2B pricing instantly.
*   **Document Generation & Status Tracking:** Completed quotations are formatted into professional digital invoices. Sales managers can track each quote through various lifecycle stages: Draft, Generated, Sent, Approved, or Archived.

### 6. Client Relationship Management (CRM)
Maintaining strong business client relationships requires centralized communication records.

*   **Client Profiles:** Comprehensive directory containing corporate names, primary contacts, email addresses, phone numbers, and geographical regions.
*   **Historical Activity:** Each client record links directly to their historical quotations, active contracts, and specific account statuses.
*   **Account Controls:** Quick actions to update client details, adjust credit limits, or flag accounts requiring urgent administrative attention.

### 7. Financial Reports & Analytics
Data-driven decision-making requires accessible, clear visualizations.

*   **Revenue Analytics:** Charts tracking monthly revenue trends, average quotation values, and high-margin product formulations.
*   **Ingredient Popularity:** Insights into which raw materials are most frequently requested across all active client quotations.
*   **Export Capabilities:** Data tables and summaries can be reviewed and filtered by specific date ranges for executive review meetings.

### 8. System Configuration & Settings
A flexible administration panel allowing organization leaders to adapt the software to changing business needs.

*   **Profile & Organization Management:** Update company contact details, tax identification numbers, and localized currency formatting.
*   **Operational Preferences:** Set default markup percentages, standard delivery timelines, and automatic reminder intervals for expiring quotations.

## Operational Workflow Example

To illustrate how these features connect during daily operations:

1.  **Supplier Intake:** A procurement officer logs in and navigates to the **Uploads** module to import a newly received supplier price sheet.
2.  **Catalog Update:** The system validates the uploaded document and instantly updates the stock pricing in the **Ingredients** catalog.
3.  **Client Request:** A business client requests a custom protein formulation. A sales manager opens the **Quotation Wizard**, selects the client from the **Clients** database, and adds the newly updated ingredients.
4.  **Instant Pricing:** The system computes exact profit margins and generates a formal price quote.
5.  **Executive Oversight:** The managing director opens the **Dashboard** on their tablet during a meeting and instantly sees the newly generated quotation reflected in the daily performance metrics.

## System Access Guide

For newly onboarded administrators and managers:

*   **Portal URL:** Navigate to your login domain.
*   **Sign In:** Enter your official administrative email address and password.
*   **Navigation:** Use the left-hand menu to switch between modules. On desktop, click the toggle icon at the top of the sidebar to collapse or expand your navigation workspace.
*   **Support & Maintenance:** For credential resets, role modifications, or system updates, contact the central Nutralike Systems Operations team.
